Hey, I'm Jennifer, a technical product marketer and engineer who brings clarity to complex deep topics, from quantum computing to cryptography to sports tech.

About

I'm a technical product marketer passionate about making complex concepts accessible, particularly in quantum computing and cryptography.

Since 2019, I've worked across software engineering and developer relations in the blockchain industry, from founding an NFT marketplace that raised $1.7 million to leading developer relations at a major blockchain contributor.

My interest in quantum computing began while researching post-quantum cryptography for blockchain. Today, I write about quantum computing almost daily, reaching over 1,000 followers across Medium and Substack.

I'm also building Openlane, a performance analytics app for Masters swimmers, as a Masters swimmer myself! The app has grown to over 4,000 users and 15,000 page views within the Masters swimming community.
